Dr. Ciyamala Kushbu S is an Associate Professor in the Department of Information Science and Engineering (ISE) at CMR Institute of Technology (CMRIT), Bangalore.

Before this role she taught for 2 years as an Assistant Professor in the ISE department at CMRIT and for 6.5 years as an Assistant Professor in the Department of ECE at RMK College of Engineering and Technology, Tamil Nadu. She also has 7 years of research experience at Anna University, Tamil Nadu.

Her contributions include more than 14 journal and conference papers, participation in more than 15 FDPs and workshops, and the book Advanced Computer Architecture published by Sahara Publications. Three of her papers are indexed in the Web of Science.

She is a university rank holder in both her B.E and M.E degrees and was school topper in the 10th and 12th grades. One of her articles won the best paper award at MIT, Chennai, and she was among the top 10 candidates for a project selected by ICTACT.
